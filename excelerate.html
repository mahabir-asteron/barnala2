Overview

What Is ExceleRATE?

ExceleRATE is a proactive driver risk evaluation designed to answer the question of driver fitness-for-duty.

How Does ExceleRATE Work?

ExceleRATE is made up of two assessments, Vitals Mobile (in office) and CORE (on road), which are performed utilizing the Impirica app on approved mobile tablets.

When done independently, Vitals Mobile and CORE provide insight into a driver's fitness for duty based on their cognitive and functional abilities to drive. When completed together, ExceleRATE provides a single, easily understood score that allows decision makers to pinpoint risk and proactively respond.

Vitals Mobile

What Is Your Road Test Not Telling You?

Driving is a complex task which requires various cognitive functions in order to maintain safety. Across 4 tablet-based tasks, Vitals Mobile measures 11 out of the 12 cognitive functions identified by the CCMTA as necessary for driving.

CORE

Commercial On-Road Evaluation

Are you still doing the same old road test? CORE is a gold-standard driving evaluation for measuring fitness and competence to drive. By adopting a cognitive science based and standardized on-road evaluation, companies can proactively identify risk and ensure safer driving.
