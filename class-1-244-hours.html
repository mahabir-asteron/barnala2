MELT Program

What Is the MELT Program?

The 244-hour Class 1 MELT (Mandatory Entry-Level Training) program is a provincial requirement for all new Class 1 commercial driver's license applicants in Manitoba. It ensures that every student receives in-depth instruction on:

  • Vehicle operation and control
  • Professional driving habits
  • Road safety and emergency response
  • Regulations and compliance
  • On-road practical training

This intensive course provides increased safety, awareness, and professionalism, preparing you for the realities of working on the road.

Career Opportunities with a Class 1 License in Manitoba

Graduates of this program are qualified to operate semi-trailer trucks, tractor-trailers, long-haul commercial vehicles, and equipment for the construction and freight industries. With a Class 1 license in Winnipeg, you'll be ready to work for transportation companies, local and cross-border freight firms, construction and resource industries, or owner-operator businesses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between the 121.5-hour and 244-hour Class 1 programs?

The 121.5-hour program is a fast-track option for students with prior experience or exemptions. The 244-hour program is the MELT-standard course required by law for most new drivers in Manitoba. It includes more classroom hours, in-depth theory, and additional behind-the-wheel practice.

Do I need a Class 5 license to start the 244-hour program?

Yes. You must hold a valid Class 5 Full license (not Learner) to register for the Class 1 MELT training.

Will this license allow me to drive across Canada?

Absolutely. A Manitoba Class 1 license is valid for interprovincial travel and recognized by most major carriers across the country.

How soon can I start working after I get my license?

Many of our graduates find employment within weeks of completing the course. We also offer job placement assistance through our local industry connections.
