Overview

What Are Air Brakes?

Air brakes are compressed air-powered braking systems used in heavy-duty vehicles like semi-trucks, buses, and trailers. Known for their power, consistency, and safety, these systems are essential for anyone looking to work in commercial driving or transportation.

Why Take Our Air Brake Endorsement Course?

Our Air Brake Endorsement Course meets all MPI (Manitoba Public Insurance) standards and prepares you to pass the Air Brake Knowledge Test with ease. Upon completion, you'll be ready to get the "S" endorsement added to your license.

Who Needs It

Who Needs Air Brake Training?

You're required to take this course if:

  • You're upgrading to a Class 1, 2, or 3 license
  • You want to drive vehicles with air brake systems
  • You're starting a new career in freight, transit, construction, or waste management
  • You are transitioning from another province or country and need Manitoba certification

Curriculum

What You'll Learn

Our Manitoba air brake course combines classroom learning with practical training. Topics include:

  • Components of the air brake system
  • Dual air brake systems and trailer brakes
  • System checks and air leak detection
  • Brake lag and brake fade
  • Safe braking practices and emergency protocols
  • Performing daily air brake inspections

Practical Training — Hands-On Learning

In addition to classroom sessions, students will practice:

  • Performing a pre-trip air brake inspection
  • Identifying system components on real vehicles
  • Reacting to system failures and pressure loss scenarios
  • Using spring brakes and parking brakes correctly

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the air brake course?

Most students complete the course in 1 to 2 days, depending on class size and scheduling.

Is the air brake course required for a Class 1 license?

Yes, it is mandatory in Manitoba for all commercial drivers operating vehicles with air brakes.

Do I need to bring anything?

Just a valid driver's license and a positive attitude. We provide all training materials and hands-on instruction.

What happens after I complete the course?

You'll receive a certificate that you can take to MPI. After passing the air brake knowledge test, the endorsement will be added to your license.
