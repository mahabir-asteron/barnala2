Student Records Policy

Certificate or Diplomas must be issued

16 A private vocational institution must give the certificate or diploma set out in the student contract to a student who successfully completes the student's program of instruction.

Transcripts must be issued

17(1) a private vocational institution must provide a student with a transcript detailing the marks or grades achieved by the student at the following times:

  1. After the student graduates or ceases to be enrolled in a program of instruction;
  2. If the student's program of instruction is longer than one year, at the end of each year of the program;
  3. Any time the student requests a transcript during the 25 years after the student graduates or ceases to be enrolled.

17(2) The institution must not charge a student a fee for a transcript provided in accordance with clauses (1)(a) or (b) but may charge a fee not more than $25 for a transcript provided in any other circumstance.

Student Record

18(2) The student records must be retained, in either paper or electronic form, for at least 25 years after the later of the date on which

  1. The institution entered the student contract; or
  2. The student ceases to be enrolled in the institution.

18(3) if the student records are retained in

  1. Paper form, the institution must
    1. Ensure that the records are stored securely in an accessible location, and
    2. On request, provide a student with their records;
  2. Electronic form, the institution must
    1. ensure the records are accessible to students and are in a format that allows for copies to be made,
    2. Back up the records in accordance with the regular schedule the institution has established for backing up electronic records,
    3. Use a third-party storage company that stores the records off-site from the private vocational institution, and
    4. establish a recovery plan for the records to be followed in the event of a disaster.
