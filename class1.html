Program Options

Two Certified Paths

Choose the program that matches your timeline and career goals. Both are recognized commercial training standards in Manitoba.

121.5 Hours

M.E.L.T. (Mandatory Entry-Level Training)

The baseline certified Class 1 program covering vehicle inspection, in-cab skills, air brakes, and on-road driving fundamentals.

Entry-Level Professional Truck Driver Training

An extended program with more behind-the-wheel time, opening up the widest range of job opportunities after graduation.
