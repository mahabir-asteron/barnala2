Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a Class 1 license in Manitoba?

With our 121.5-hour program, you can complete your training in just a few weeks, depending on your schedule and availability.

Is the 121.5-hour program MELT-approved?

This program is designed for those who meet the exemption criteria or already have prior training. If you need a full MELT course, we also offer the 244-hour version.

Can I work outside Manitoba with a Class 1 license?

Yes. A Manitoba Class 1 license is recognized across Canada, and many employers in other provinces accept it for long-haul and interprovincial transport.

What's the difference between this and the longer program?

The 121.5-hour course is streamlined for those looking for a quicker path into the industry, while the 244-hour MELT course is mandatory for new drivers without prior experience or exemptions.
