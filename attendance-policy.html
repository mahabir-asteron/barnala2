Student Policy

Attendance Policy

All students enrolled in the Class 1 Training Program are required to complete the full mandatory training hours for their selected course (121.5 hours or 244 hours), in accordance with applicable training standards and regulatory requirements.

Attendance is recorded based on actual hours completed. Students must attend and actively participate in all scheduled classroom, in-yard, and in-vehicle training sessions for the full duration of each session.

Absence & Notification

Students must provide a minimum of 24 hours’ notice if they are unable to attend a scheduled session. Failure to provide proper notice will be considered a “no-show.”

Missed Training & Make-Up Hours

All missed hours must be completed to meet the required total program hours. Make-up sessions are subject to instructor, vehicle, and schedule availability. Additional fees may apply for rescheduling missed sessions, particularly in cases of late cancellation or no-show.

Late Arrival & Early Departure

Students arriving late or leaving early will only be credited for the actual time attended. Lost time must be rescheduled and completed.

No-Show Policy

Students who fail to attend a scheduled session without prior notice may forfeit that session and may be charged a rescheduling fee.

Completion Requirement

Students who do not complete the full required hours (121.5 or 244 hours) will not be issued a course completion certificate and will be ineligible for program completion.

Non-Compliance

The school reserves the right to suspend or terminate training for students demonstrating:

  • Repeated absences or no-shows
  • Excessive missed hours
  • Lack of participation or failure to progress
