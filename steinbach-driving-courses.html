FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What’s the difference between 121.5 and 244-hour Class 1 programs?

A: 121.5 Hrs is a focused program for quick job entry; 244 Hrs offers more practical experience and higher employment chances.

Q2: Do I need an air brake course with Class 1 or 3?

A: Yes, air brake endorsement is mandatory for both Class 1 and Class 3 license holders in Manitoba. We offer both 121.5-hour and 244-hour certified programs.

Q3: Is the driving training the same in all three locations?

A: Yes. We maintain consistent curriculum and training quality across Winnipeg, Portage, and Steinbach.

Q4: Can new immigrants enroll in Class 5 or Class 1 courses?

A: Absolutely. We have special support and guidance for newcomers to help them adapt and get licensed fast.

Q5: Are safety courses included in Class 1 training?

A: Yes, courses like HOS and Load Securement are integrated or available as add-ons based on your selected plan.

Q6: What’s the age requirement to apply for truck driver training?

A: You must be at least 18 and hold a valid Manitoba Class 5 Full license.

Q7: Do you help with job placements?

A: Yes, we connect students with our trucking company partners for job interviews and placement support.

Q8: Can I practice before my license test?

A: Yes, we offer additional practice sessions and mock tests to build your confidence.

Q9: Are your instructors certified?

A: Every instructor at Barnala is certified and experienced in real-world trucking or driving instruction.

Q10: Do you offer refresher or brush-up courses?

A: Yes, for both commercial and personal licenses. Ideal if you’re re-entering the industry.
