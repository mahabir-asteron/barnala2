Overview

What Is a Class 3 License in Manitoba?

A Class 3 license in Manitoba allows you to operate:

  • Single motor vehicles with three or more axles
  • Large commercial trucks (excluding semi-trailers)
  • Combination vehicles like trucks towing trailers or other vehicles under 4,600 kg

It's essential for drivers in industries like:

  • Construction & Infrastructure
  • Public Works & Utilities
  • Waste Management
  • Local Delivery Services
  • Fleet Operations

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the Class 3 course in Manitoba?

Our program is approximately 21 hours of focused training. It includes vehicle familiarization, safety checks, driving practice, and Class 3 license test preparation.

What's the minimum age to apply for a Class 3 license in Manitoba?

You must be at least 18 years old and hold a valid Class 5 Full license.

Is the Manitoba Class 3 license recognized in other provinces?

Yes. While some provinces may have additional rules, a Class 3 license from Manitoba is widely accepted across Canada.

Do you help with job placement after the course?

Yes, we work with several industry employers in Winnipeg, connecting qualified graduates with immediate job opportunities.
