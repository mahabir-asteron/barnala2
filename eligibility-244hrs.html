Eligibility Requirements

Here's what you'll need to qualify for the 244-hour Class 1 MELT program.

Age

Generally, you must be at least 18 years old to apply for a Class 1 license in Manitoba.

Air Brake Endorsement

Investing more time in your training can lead to better job performance, higher earning potential, and greater job satisfaction in the long run.

Driver's License

You need a valid Class 5 (full) driver's license or its equivalent.

Medical Fitness

You must pass a medical examination to ensure you meet the physical requirements for driving a commercial vehicle.

Language Proficiency

Proficiency in English or French is typically required to understand road signs, safety regulations, and communicate effectively.

Criminal Record Check

A clean criminal record check may be required, as individuals with certain criminal convictions may not be eligible for a Class 1 license.

Knowledge Test

You must pass a written knowledge test on traffic laws, safe driving practices, and specific regulations related to Class 1 vehicles.
